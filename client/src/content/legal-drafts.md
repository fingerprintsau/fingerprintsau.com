You must not:

- **Limits on our liability.** To the extent the law allows:

## 5. Prohibited Items Policy

**Last updated:** [date]

You can't list:
- **Electronics** (Fingerprints doesn't accept electronics at this time).
- **Counterfeit, replica or unauthorised copies** of branded goods.
- **Weapons:** firearms, ammunition, knives restricted in Victoria, and weapon parts.
- **Drugs and related items:** drugs, drug paraphernalia, tobacco and vapes, alcohol, and prescription medicines.
- **Unsafe goods:** recalled or banned products, including items subject to mandatory safety standards that don't comply (for example, some children's products).
- **Stolen goods,** or items you don't have the right to sell.
- **Personal data, accounts, gift cards and vouchers.**
- **Harmful or offensive items:** adult content, hate symbols and offensive material.
- **Protected wildlife products,** and animal parts restricted by law.
- **Used underwear or swimwear bottoms** without hygiene liners [CONFIRM category rule].
- **Services, and "mystery" listings** that hide what's being sold.

**Large items:** allowed, but marked "Pickup only" or with the seller's own delivery arrangements.

We may remove listings and suspend accounts that break this policy, and report illegal activity to authorities.
